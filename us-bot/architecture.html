Daily Schedule

03:50 UTC · 09:20 AM IST
IB Gateway Restart
Cron restarts Docker container before IBKR daily reset (03:45 UTC). Second restart at 04:20 UTC as safety net. Bot restarts at 04:30 UTC with fresh connection.
13:36 UTC · 07:06 PM IST
Morning Scan
Fetches 3 months OHLCV for all 51 symbols from IBKR. Computes gap%, prev-day volume ratio, ATR, MA. Checks market-wide filter. Fires spot + F&O entries on qualifying signals. Results saved to scan_log.json and sent via Telegram.
16:00 UTC · 09:30 PM IST
Midday Exit Check
Checks all open spot and F&O positions against trailing ATR stop and time exit. Sells if triggered.
19:45 UTC · 01:15 AM IST
Pre-close Exit Check
Final exit check before US market close. Increments hold day counter for all open positions.
19:50 UTC · 01:20 AM IST
EOD Summary → Telegram + Email
Portfolio value, cash balance, all open positions with unrealized P&L. Sent to Telegram and email.
